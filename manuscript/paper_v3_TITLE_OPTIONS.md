# Paper v3 — Title Options

## Selection criteria
- Centers the strongest actual result (dispersion / fan-out / loss of coherence)
- Does not mislead readers into expecting centroid displacement
- Avoids overclaiming "collapse" unless carefully justified
- Concise enough for a journal title line
- Biologically grounded, not method-centric

---

## Conservative specialized-journal titles

1. **Dispersion-dominated loss of alveolar epithelial state coherence in lethal COVID-19**
   - Pro: precise, directly states the finding
   - Con: "dispersion-dominated" may sound too statistical for biology journals

2. **COVID-19 fans alveolar epithelial cells along a continuous injury axis: trajectory analysis with ablation-based robustness validation**
   - Pro: vivid, conveys the fan-out biology
   - Con: long; "fans" is informal for some venues

3. **Loss of transcriptomic coherence, not coherent displacement, characterizes alveolar epithelial injury in lethal COVID-19**
   - Pro: explicitly distinguishes the two competing models
   - Con: negative framing in a title can be risky

4. **Alveolar epithelial heterogeneity expansion in lethal COVID-19: evidence from trajectory analysis, ablation robustness, and cross-cohort replication**
   - Pro: emphasizes heterogeneity, mentions replication
   - Con: wordy

## Broader-interest titles

5. **Lethal COVID-19 fragments the alveolar epithelial state landscape rather than displacing it**
   - Pro: vivid, conveys the key insight concisely
   - Con: "fragments" may overstate

6. **Fan-out, not displacement: the geometry of alveolar epithelial failure in lethal COVID-19**
   - Pro: memorable, model-distinguishing
   - Con: "geometry" may confuse non-computational readers

7. **Loss of epithelial state coherence as a portable signature of severe alveolar injury**
   - Pro: generalizes beyond COVID, emphasizes portability
   - Con: does not mention COVID (the actual study system)

8. **Alveolar epithelial cells in lethal COVID-19 lose state coherence rather than shifting to a uniform damaged state**
   - Pro: biologically clear, distinguishes models
   - Con: long

## Best balanced titles

9. **Dispersion, not displacement: loss of alveolar epithelial state coherence in lethal COVID-19**
   - Pro: concise, model-distinguishing, centers the strongest finding
   - Con: "dispersion" may need context

10. **Alveolar epithelial state coherence loss in lethal COVID-19: a dispersion-dominated injury geometry replicated across cohorts**
    - Pro: includes replication, precise
    - Con: slightly long

---

## Final choice for paper_v3

**Title:** Dispersion, not displacement: loss of alveolar epithelial state coherence in lethal COVID-19

**Rationale:**
- Centers the actual strongest finding (dispersion/fan-out)
- Explicitly signals the negative result (displacement failed) as a conceptual contribution
- "State coherence loss" is more precise and defensible than "robustness collapse"
- Short enough for any journal format
- The colon structure makes it scannable
- A reviewer reading only the title knows exactly what the paper claims and what it does not
