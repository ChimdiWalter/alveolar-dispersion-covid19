# Abstract

The alveolar epithelium sustains gas exchange through the coordinated function of type I (AT1) and type II (AT2) cells, with AT2 cells serving as the resident progenitors for injury repair. In lethal COVID-19, these cells face concurrent viral assault via ACE2-mediated entry and inflammatory collateral damage, yet whether their failure proceeds as a binary event or as a staged, continuous process has not been resolved at single-cell resolution using trajectory-based approaches.

Here, we analyzed 22,128 alveolar epithelial cells (9,608 AT1, 11,341 AT2, and 1,179 ECM-high transitional cells) from the Columbia/NYP COVID-19 Lung Atlas (SCP1219), comprising 12,181 nuclei from 7 healthy donors and 9,947 nuclei from 20 COVID-19 donors. We constructed a transcriptomic manifold (Seurat-v3 highly variable genes, PCA, k-nearest-neighbor graph, UMAP, diffusion map), computed diffusion pseudotime rooted at the healthy AT2 centroid, and scored each cell for eight curated gene programs spanning identity, stress, repair, and cell death. Ten pre-specified ablation analyses stress-tested every major analytical choice.

COVID-19 cells were shifted toward higher pseudotime than controls (median 0.111 vs. 0.063; observed difference +0.048; permutation p = 0.001, N = 1,000). However, the primary analysis did **not** support directional displacement from the healthy PCA centroid (Mann-Whitney U, one-sided COVID > Healthy, p = 1.0; rank-biserial r = 0.14). Instead, COVID-19 cells showed markedly greater within-group dispersion in PCA space (COVID variance 33.3 vs. healthy 14.6; Levene's test p < 10⁻³⁰), and an ECM-high transitional population expressing KRT8/CLDN4 was enriched 3.1-fold in COVID-19 (8.5% of COVID cells vs. 2.7% of control cells). Gene programs ordered along pseudotime with AT2 identity peaking first (pseudotime 0.01), AT1 identity second (0.27), and injury programs (apoptosis, AT2→AT1 differentiation, NF-κB, oxidative stress, interferon, senescence) concentrated in the upper half of pseudotime (0.59–0.94). The program ordering was stable across four alternative pseudotime roots (median Spearman ρ = 0.55) and under curated-only gene programs (ρ = 1.0) but degraded under MSigDB-only programs (ρ = −0.80) and a COVID-extreme root (ρ = −0.10). Leave-one-donor-out analysis preserved the direction of the pseudotime shift in all 27 iterations (range 0.028–0.202; median ≈ 0.063).

Of three pre-registered criteria for epithelial robustness collapse (centroid displacement, dispersion, pseudotime enrichment), two were supported (dispersion and pseudotime), one was not (centroid displacement), yielding a "moderate" composite assessment. These results support a model in which lethal COVID-19 fans alveolar epithelial cells along a continuous injury axis with increased heterogeneity — rather than displacing them coherently as a block — and in which a KRT8⁺ transitional population accumulates in a mid-trajectory state consistent with stalled AT2→AT1 differentiation.
