* Abstract

The alveolar epithelium sustains gas exchange through the coordinated function of AT1 and AT2 cells, with AT2 cells serving as the resident progenitors for injury repair. In lethal COVID-19, these cells face concurrent viral assault (ACE2-mediated entry) and inflammatory collateral damage. We analyzed 22,128 alveolar epithelial cells (9,608 AT1; 11,341 AT2; 1,179 ECM-high transitional) from the Columbia/NYP COVID-19 Lung Atlas (SCP1219): 12,181 nuclei from 7 healthy donors and 9,947 from 20 COVID-19 donors. We constructed a transcriptomic manifold (Seurat-v3 HVGs, PCA, k-NN, UMAP, diffusion map), computed diffusion pseudotime rooted at the healthy AT2 centroid, and scored each cell for eight curated gene programs. Ten pre-specified ablations stress-tested every major analytical choice.

COVID-19 cells were shifted toward higher pseudotime (median 0.111 vs. 0.063; observed difference +0.048; permutation /p/ = 0.001, /N/ = 1000). The primary analysis did *not* support directional centroid displacement (one-sided Mann--Whitney /U/, COVID > Healthy, /p/ = 1.0; rank-biserial /r/ = 0.14). COVID-19 cells showed substantially greater within-group dispersion (variance 33.3 vs. 14.6; Levene /p/ < 10^{-30}). A KRT8^{+}/CLDN4^{+} transitional population was enriched 3.1-fold in COVID-19 (8.5% vs. 2.7%). Program ordering was stable under curated-only programs (Spearman ρ = 1.0) but inverted under MSigDB-only programs (ρ = -0.80). Leave-one-donor-out preserved the pseudotime-shift direction in all 27 iterations (range 0.028-0.202). Two of three pre-registered criteria were supported (dispersion, pseudotime); one was not (centroid displacement). Composite: *moderate*.

* Introduction

Every breath depends on a thin layer of cells deep in the lung: the alveolar epithelium, composed of AT1 cells (flat; gas-exchange barrier) and AT2 cells (cuboidal; surfactant production and resident stem cells). When AT1 cells are lost, AT2 cells differentiate through a KRT8^{+}/CLDN4^{+}/SFN^{+} transitional state (Barkauskas 2013; Kobayashi 2020; Strunz 2020).

In lethal COVID-19, SARS-CoV-2 enters AT2 cells via ACE2 (Ziegler 2020; Hou 2020); dysregulated inflammation damages the epithelium from both directions. Cluster-based analyses of COVID-19 lung atlases (Melms 2021; Delorey 2021) force continuous biology into discrete bins. A trajectory-based approach --- treating each cell's expression profile as a position on a transcriptomic manifold, ordering cells by pseudotime (Haghverdi 2016; Setty 2019) --- can in principle resolve staged failure. Here we apply such an approach, with systematic ablation-based validation, to the alveolar compartment of SCP1219 (Melms 2021), and frame the observed patterns as /epithelial robustness collapse/.

* Results

** Cohort composition

22,128 alveolar epithelial cells (9,608 AT1; 11,341 AT2; 1,179 ECM-high) from 7 healthy donors (12,181 nuclei) and 20 COVID-19 donors (9,947 nuclei). Per-donor counts 62-2,850; 10/27 donors < 300 cells. Marker validation: AT2 SFTPC/SFTPA1, AT1 AGER/HOPX, ECM-high KRT8/CLDN4 (Table S3). ECM-high 3.1-fold enriched in COVID-19 (8.5% vs. 2.7%; χ² /p/ < 10^{-30}).

** Manifold and pseudotime

Normalization → log1p → 3,000 HVGs (Seurat v3) → scale → 50-PC PCA → k-NN (k=15) → UMAP, diffusion map. Harmony batch correction was configured but *failed at runtime* (harmonypy returned shape (50,) instead of (22128, 50)); pipeline fell back to uncorrected PCA, and Ablation 3 explicitly tests this fallback. Diffusion pseudotime rooted at cell nearest healthy-AT2 centroid in diffusion-component space, min-max normalized to [0, 1].

** Centroid displacement: NOT supported

One-sided Mann--Whitney /U/ (COVID > Healthy) on distance-to-healthy-centroid in PCA-50: /U/ = 5.21×10^7, /p/ = 1.0. Means COVID 12.36 vs. Healthy 12.10; medians COVID 10.68 vs. Healthy 11.56 (*COVID median lower*). Rank-biserial /r/ = 0.14 opposite to alternative. Pre-specified core test failed.

** Dispersion: IS supported

Within-group variance COVID 33.28 vs. Healthy 14.58 (2.3×). Levene statistic 276.5; /p/ < 10^{-30}.

* Discussion

** Principal finding

COVID-19 fans alveolar cells into a heterogeneous cloud --- same rough center, broader tail --- rather than moving the population coherently. The signal is /loss of coherence/, not directional motion.

** Why centroid displacement failed and dispersion succeeded

Median COVID distance to healthy centroid (10.68) was /lower/ than median healthy distance to healthy centroid (11.56). A uniform-displacement test averages the near-healthy COVID subpopulation against the distant COVID subpopulation and sees nothing. Dispersion captures exactly this structure. Future robustness-collapse studies should use variance/trajectory metrics.

** Program ordering

Data do not support a specific IFN→NF-κB→repair→apoptosis cascade. Fine ordering sensitive to gene-list (Ablation 8 MSigDB ρ = -0.80) and to root (Ablation 5 COVID-extreme ρ = -0.10). Identity-first, injury-second pattern is stable; the detailed injury-program sequence is hypothesis-generating, not confirmed.

** Trajectory is alveolar-specific

Ablation 6 collapses the shift when airway epithelium is included. The trajectory is a property of the alveolar compartment only; generalizing to lung epithelium requires compartment-specific trajectories.

** Limitations

Cross-sectional; single dataset (no replication); lethal cases only; post-mortem effects; donor imbalance (10/27 donors < 300 cells); fine ordering sensitive to gene lists; *Harmony failed → Ablation 3 cannot distinguish corrected from uncorrected*; *Palantir not installed → no orthogonal trajectory method*; *GPX1 silently dropped from oxidative-stress program* --- other programs may have analogous dropouts.

** Conclusion

Lethal COVID-19 does not push alveolar epithelium coherently away from the healthy state. It fans cells along a continuous pseudotime axis and increases heterogeneity. 2/3 pre-registered criteria supported (moderate composite). Pseudotime shift robust; fine program ordering hypothesis-generating; trajectory compartment-specific.

* Methods (abridged)

- Data: SCP1219 (Melms 2021), 116,313 nuclei; retained AT1/AT2/ECM-high alveolar epithelial (n = 22,128).
- Preprocessing: =sc.pp.normalize_total(1e4)= → =log1p= → 3,000 Seurat-v3 HVGs → =scale(max=10)= → 50-PC PCA.
- Graph: k-NN k=15; UMAP min_dist=0.3; 15-component diffusion map.
- Harmony: configured (donor_id, 20 iter, sigma=0.1); harmonypy 0.2.0 returned shape (50,); pipeline fell back to uncorrected PCA.
- Pseudotime: =sc.tl.dpt= rooted at cell nearest healthy-AT2 centroid in diffusion space; normalized to [0,1].
- Programs: 8 programs scored with =sc.tl.score_genes= against =.raw=.
- Tests: one-sided Mann--Whitney /U/ (displacement); Levene (dispersion); 1000-permutation median-difference (pseudotime); Spearman ρ (program ordering).
- Software: Python 3.12.3; scanpy 1.12.1; anndata 0.12.10; numpy 2.4.3; scipy 1.17.1; pandas 2.3.3; sklearn 1.8.0; matplotlib 3.10.8; harmonypy 0.2.0. Seeds fixed to 42.
